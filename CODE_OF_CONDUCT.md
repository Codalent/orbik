# Code of conduct

Orbik is built in public by people who disagree, work in different languages and know different
amounts. This is how we treat each other in its issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
space that belongs to the project.

## What we expect

- Be considerate and patient. Assume good faith, and ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Criticise work, not people. Say what is wrong with a change and what would fix it.
- Make room for people who are new here; a question is never a nuisance.
- Accept a maintainer's decision about the project's direction even when you would have decided
  differently, and say so if you disagree, once and civilly.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, and personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately derailing a discussion, or repeating an argument after it has been decided.

## What happens

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this
code, and may warn, suspend or ban the person responsible, from any of the project's spaces,
for as long as they judge right. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is
not shared without their consent.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to the maintainers privately. Until a dedicated address is
listed here, use the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability** and say that it
is a conduct report; it reaches the maintainers alone.

This code applies to the project's spaces and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
